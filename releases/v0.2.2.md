## Recorded changes

- Shared custom instructions and working habits span Workspace, Studio and Blueprint.
- Searchable skills gain review drafts, per-agent preferences and scanned Hermes imports.
- Bounded history retains original requests and recent corrections; engineering guidance is bundled for new installs.
- Provider-reported usage/cost is distinguished from local, subscription or unknown billing. Update recommendations require a configured signed feed.
- Settings and activity titles become clearer; removed inactive approval/scheduler surfaces preserve existing records.
- Fixes cover rapid-message IDs, builder/reviewer ordering, final rejection, terminal restoration and exact registered command arguments.
- Validated settings import/export and honest post-apply scan reporting protect existing work; packaged resources no longer depend on a developer checkout.
