# 0.2.36

License keys are activated with the Imperium website, where they are sold. A key saved on this PC stays in the user profile through updates, and the app does not delete or replace it.

A key already active on another PC is refused there, and copying the license file does not unlock a different computer. A key from a refunded or disputed purchase stops opening the app at the next start. The app will not run when this PC does not have a key.

Settings → Privacy & policies explains what license activation sends to the Imperium website and what it keeps.

Existing installations can update from the app or with the website update check. A computer without Imperium is directed to buy a license.
