Source-record date: **2026-09-10**. This is the date recorded in the publisher's note, not an inferred public upload date.

Historical note record. Availability of an archived binary is not asserted.

## Recorded changes

- Project-specific findings support confirmed Quarantine, Delete and Ignore actions, with preserved-file recovery.
- Main-folder repair retains patch and rollback history; optional Windows antimalware inspection is available where permitted.
- Checks distinguish genuine findings from incomplete coverage. Ordinary Clone edits or no-patch states are not incidents.
- Red-team results describe current inspected content and concrete boundary checks; automatic project-opening rescan prompts were removed.
- Background helper/model/firewall/cleanup launches use no-console behavior.
- Stale/wrong-project actions are refused; partial quarantine remains visible. Recognized renames update Main location and moved folders can be reviewed/reconnected.

## Data and provenance

Normal installer upgrades replace program files and preserve app data, Main projects and scratch work. Keep independent backups; uninstalling is not complete erasure.
